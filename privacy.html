1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy covers scnf-foundation.org (the “Site”), published by the Sir Chris Nkwonta Foundation (the “Foundation,” “we,” “us”), a non-profit organisation based in Obehie, Ukwa West LGA, Abia State, Nigeria. It explains what information the Site collects, how it is used, what cookies and third-party services are involved, and the choices and rights available to you as a visitor.

This policy applies only to this website. It does not cover information the Foundation may collect offline, such as during in-person scholarship applications, bursary awards, or community outreach events.

2. Information We Collect

Information you choose to provide

The Contact & Support page includes a message form asking for your name, email address, the reason for your enquiry, and your message. When you press “Send Message,” the Site does not transmit this information to any server we control. Instead, it opens your own device’s default email application with the details pre-filled, and the email is sent directly from your account to ours, the same as if you had written to us yourself. We only receive and process this information if you go on to actually send that email.

Information collected automatically

This Site does not run any analytics, advertising, or audience-measurement scripts, so we do not automatically log or profile your visit ourselves. The only automatic data exchange comes from the embedded third-party services described in Section 3 and Section 6 below — for example, your device’s IP address is visible to Google when it loads a typeface or a map, in the same way it would be visible to any server your browser contacts.

3. Cookies & Similar Technologies

A cookie is a small file a website can ask your browser to store. This Site does not set any cookies of its own, and we do not use cookies for analytics or advertising. The table below lists every cookie-adjacent technology actually present on the Site.

Service Provider Purpose Type & timing
Cookie preference This website (browser storage) Remembers your cookie choice so we don’t ask again on your next visit Strictly necessary — saved only after you choose an option below
Google Maps embed Google LLC Displays an interactive map of our Obehie office on the Contact page Third-party, functional — loaded only after you click “Load Map,” or if you choose “Accept All” below
Google Fonts Google LLC Loads the Fraunces, Work Sans, IBM Plex Mono, and Fuzzy Bubbles typefaces used across the Site Third-party resource, no cookie set — your IP address is sent to Google on every page load
Unsplash images Unsplash Inc. Provides stock photography used as placeholder imagery on some pages Third-party resource, no cookie set — your IP address is sent to Unsplash when those images load

When you first visit, a banner lets you choose Accept All (loads the Google Maps embed automatically wherever it appears) or Necessary Only (keeps it as a click-to-load placeholder, so its cookie is set only if you press “Load Map” yourself). No option is pre-selected, and continuing to browse or scroll the Site is never treated as consent.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Respond to messages sent through the Contact & Support page;
  • Display the Site’s pages, typefaces, imagery, and map correctly in your browser;
  • Remember your cookie preference so you are not asked again.

We do not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (“NDPA”) and its General Application and Implementation Directive (“GAID”), we rely on:

  • Your consent — for the Google Maps embed, given either by pressing “Accept All” in the cookie banner or by clicking “Load Map” directly;
  • Performance of your request — when you send us a message through the Contact & Support page, we process the resulting email to reply to you;
  • Legitimate interest — for loading Google Fonts and Unsplash imagery, which is necessary to display the Site’s design and is limited to what is needed for that purpose.

6. Third-Party Services

The Site embeds or loads resources from the following third-party providers. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google (Fonts and Maps) — see Google’s own privacy policy for how it handles data these services collect.
  • Unsplash — see Unsplash’s own privacy policy for how it handles data its image CDN collects.

We do not control, and are not responsible for, how these providers process data once it reaches their servers.

7. Data Sharing & International Transfers

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. The only routine transfer of data outside Nigeria happens when your browser contacts Google or Unsplash directly, as described above — the Foundation itself does not relay your data to them. Any message you choose to email us stays within your own email provider and ours.

8. Data Retention

We do not store contact-form submissions on this website or on servers we control, since the form hands off to your email application rather than submitting to us directly. Emails you send us are kept in our inbox for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and, where relevant, act on your enquiry. Your cookie preference is kept in your own browser’s local storage until you clear your browser data or change your choice.

9. Your Rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and why;
  • Request access to, or a copy of, that data;
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer have a valid reason to keep;
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including by reopening the cookie banner above;
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your rights have been infringed.

To exercise any of these rights regarding an email you have sent us, contact us using the details in Section 13.

10. Children’s Privacy

This Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the Site. If you believe a child has sent us information via the Contact & Support page, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Security

Because this Site does not operate a database of visitor or form data, there is no server-side store of your information for us to secure or that could be breached through the Site itself. Security of any message you send us afterwards depends on the email providers involved, as with any email.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Site changes — for example, if we add a new embedded service. We will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page whenever we do, and material changes to how cookies are used will prompt the consent banner to reappear.
