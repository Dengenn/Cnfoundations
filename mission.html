Why We Exist

Mission & Vision

The conviction behind everything the Foundation does.

Mission

To advance human capacity building by providing education, healthcare, skills acquisition, and empowerment to indigent and underserved people of Ukwa land and beyond.

Vision

A Ukwa community where no one's talent or future is limited by a lack of means — where opportunity is within reach of every determined young person and every family in need.

Our Values
Compassion Integrity Community Excellence Giving Back
Sir Chris Nkwonta Foundation

Weaving opportunity into every life we touch across Ukwa East and Ukwa West, Abia State, Nigeria.
