What We Do

Services & Areas of Support

The Foundation's programmes fall into six broad areas of intervention across Ukwa East and Ukwa West.

Education & Scholarships

Bursaries and full scholarships from secondary school through university, including sponsorship for study abroad.

Healthcare Access

Free medical treatment for indigent families, delivered locally and, where needed, abroad.

Skills Acquisition & Empowerment

Vocational training and equipment for youths to build sustainable livelihoods.

Community Infrastructure

Support such as electric transformers for community power supply and rehabilitation of failed roads.

Youth & Sports Development

Sponsorship of academic quiz competitions, school band sets, and athletic development for young people.

Disability Support

Assistance and rehabilitation support for physically challenged persons across Ukwa land.
